Porffor patches move from anchored string edits to a git patch series, and the toolchain supports one Porffor alpha at a time (alpha 24). `porffor-patches/` is a `git format-patch` series of 26 patches against upstream `1b8225e` (one per pass, each with what, why, its class and the upstream issue); `scripts/porffor-patches.ts` applies it (`git am --3way`), exports it, and builds the patched tarball. `vendor/porffor-patched.tar.gz` ships the already-patched tree with `vendor/porffor-patched.json` (upstream commit, series hash, sha256): `ensurePorfforA21` verifies the sha256 and unpacks it, and no longer patches on the user's machine (no git needed there). The tree is identical to what the old chain produced on alpha 24 (`diff -r`). A CI job re-runs apply, export and vendor and compares the patches, the tar and the tarball byte for byte.

Removed: the alpha 21, 22 and 23 pins (`PORFFOR_A21_*`, `PORFFOR_A22_*`, `PORFFOR_A23_*`), their vendored tarballs, `ensurePorfforPatchedA21` and every `patch…A21` export, `channelOfTree`, `pinOfTree`, `A21_RELEASED_FINGERPRINT` and `a21SourceFingerprint`. `A21_PATCH_FINGERPRINT` is now the series hash. `SPROUTBOAT_PORFFOR_CHANNEL` accepts `alpha-24` (default) and the frozen `alpha-19`; `alpha-21`, `alpha-22` and `alpha-23` are an error that says they are no longer supported. `ensurePorfforA21` no longer takes `fetcher` or `timeoutMs` (nothing is downloaded). The alpha 19 line (`src/pin.ts`, `patch.ts`, `strings.ts`, its vendored tarball) is untouched.
